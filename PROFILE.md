## 1. Company Overview

**CyberCraft Pvt. Ltd.** is an **Innovation Technology Company** working across three pillars: **Information Technology, Telecommunications and Innovation Technology**.

Founded in **2007** in **Lahore, Pakistan**, CyberCraft has spent more than 17 years helping organizations computerize, automate and transform digitally. In **2024** the company was incorporated as **CyberCraft Pvt. Ltd.**, which opened its next chapter as a full-spectrum technology partner with a presence in **Pakistan, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, the United Kingdom, the Czech Republic and Canada**.

CyberCraft is one partner that can **consult, build, run and secure** a client's entire technology estate:

- **Software and ERP**: our own **CyberERP** platform, custom web, mobile and desktop applications, and implementation of Microsoft Dynamics 365, SAP and Oracle
- **Infrastructure and telecom**: data centers, networks, fiber, IP communications and IP surveillance
- **Managed IT**: complete IT outsourcing, resident engineers and 24/7 support at competitive cost
- **Cloud**: hosting and cloud services through our brand **b2cloud.io**
- **Cybersecurity**: audits, compliance and managed security operations
- **Supply**: software licenses and IT hardware
- **Customer experience**: contact center services enhanced with AI
- **Innovation**: solar and clean energy, industrial and home automation, embedded systems, IoT, AI and future technologies

CyberCraft delivers through its own teams and a network of **joint-venture (JV) partners**, which lets it take on projects of any size while keeping costs competitive.

## 3. Our Story

CyberCraft started in 2007 with a simple idea: **help businesses work better with technology.** Our first work was bringing computers, networks and software into offices across Lahore.

As our clients grew, so did we. Computerization led to **automation**. Automation led to **ERP**, and our own platform, **CyberERP**, became the core of our business. ERP projects led us into **data centers, networks and infrastructure**. Running that infrastructure for clients made us a **managed services provider**, with resident engineers working inside client organizations and, in many cases, CyberCraft running a client's entire IT function.

Along the way we added **cybersecurity**, **cloud hosting** (through our brand **b2cloud.io**), **IP communications and surveillance**, **software licensing and hardware supply**, and **contact center services**.

In **2024** we incorporated as **CyberCraft Pvt. Ltd.** and set a new direction as **An Innovation Technology Company**. Our work now spans IT, telecom and the technologies that will shape the next decade: **clean energy, automation, embedded systems, IoT and artificial intelligence**. We have also expanded internationally with a presence in the **UAE, Saudi Arabia, the UK, the Czech Republic and Canada**.

| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|

| **2007 onward** | Computerization, networking and business software for local businesses |
| **Growth years** | CyberERP developed; ERP implementations for Dynamics 365, SAP and Oracle |
| **Growth years** | Data center, network, IP surveillance and IP telephony projects |
| **Growth years** | Managed IT services and resident engineer model launched |
