Pillar 02

Cyber Security Compliance & Audit

We secure it and prove it.

Independent, honest assessment against the standards your regulators, customers and board expect, followed by hands-on help to close the gaps. When we also manage your systems, a separate team performs the audit.

Lead productCyberCraft SecureAudit · Comply · SOC · Shield · Aware · vCISO

Audit & assessment

Security audits, gap assessments and VAPT for network, web, mobile, API, cloud and wireless. Red teaming, hardening, code and ITGC reviews.

Compliance readiness

Gap assessment, remediation roadmap, policies, evidence collection, internal audit and support during certification and inspection.

Governance, risk & privacy

Risk registers, business continuity and DR, privacy programs and DPIAs, awareness training and a virtual CISO for board reporting.

Managed security

24/7 SOC and SIEM, incident response and forensics, EDR, email, firewall, MFA, PAM and DLP, and vulnerability management.

Tauseef AhmedFounder & CEO, CyberCraft Pvt. Ltd.

"Every organization now asks two questions: is our technology secure, and can we prove it? We exist to answer both, and to invent what comes next."

When I started CyberCraft in 2007, our goal was simple: help businesses use technology to work better. Over the years our clients trusted us to keep running what we built. That trust is the heart of our managed services.

Becoming CyberCraft Pvt. Ltd. in 2024, and expanding to the UAE, KSA, UK, Czech Republic and Canada, is a new chapter. What we stand for has not changed: accountability, honest advice and real value for every client.
